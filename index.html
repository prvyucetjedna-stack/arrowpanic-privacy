Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026 · com.donynko.arrowpanic

In short

  • No account and no servers of our own: your game progress stays on your phone.
  • The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may process your advertising ID and IP address. Personalised ads are shown only with your consent.
  • You can change your ad consent at any time in the game: Settings → Privacy options.

01Who is responsible

The app Arrow Panic! (package com.donynko.arrowpanic) is developed and published by MR and HS Devs, an independent developer based in Slovakia, who is the data controller for the app. Contact: prvyucetjedna@gmail.com.

For advertising, Google Ireland Limited (Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) processes data as an independent controller under its own privacy policy.

02Data stored only on your device

The game saves your progress (unlocked levels, stars, best times, hints) and your settings (language, sound, music, vibration) locally on your device. We do not receive this data and we do not run any servers for the game. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include this data in your device backup with Google. The data is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

03Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is financed by full-screen ads shown between levels through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). To show, measure and protect ads, the SDK may collect:

  • device identifiers: advertising ID, app set ID
  • IP address, from which an approximate location can be derived
  • device and app information: model, operating system, language, app version
  • ad interactions (impressions, clicks) and diagnostic data (crashes, performance)

Google uses this data to deliver ads, limit how often you see them, measure their performance, prevent fraud and, only with your consent, personalise ads. We receive only aggregated statistics, never data that identifies you.

More: How Google uses information from apps that use its services · Google Privacy Policy

04Your consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

On first launch, players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland see a consent form from Google’s User Messaging Platform, a consent management platform certified under the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework. You choose whether Google may use your data for personalised ads and store or access information on your device.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in the game: Settings → Privacy options (gear icon). Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that took place before. Without consent you may see non-personalised or limited ads. Your choice is stored on your device.

05Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and national rules implementing Art. 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive): personalised ads and storing or accessing information on your device.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): showing non-personalised ads to finance the free game, ad measurement, fraud prevention and security, as far as Google’s consent framework allows. You can object at any time.

06Other features

  • Share: the Share button creates an image of your result on your device and opens the Android share menu. You decide where to send it, for example to TikTok. We do not receive the image; the privacy policy of the app you choose applies.
  • Ratings: the game may show the Google Play in-app review window, which is provided entirely by Google Play.
  • Permissions: the game uses only internet access (for ads) and vibration. It does not access your location (GPS), camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

07Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are younger than the age at which you can consent to data processing in your country (between 13 and 16 depending on the EU country), ask a parent or guardian before agreeing to personalised ads. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us.

08Transfers outside the EU

Google may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. For these transfers Google relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

09How long data is kept

Data on your device: until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Data collected by Google: according to Google’s retention rules.

10Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification and erasure, the right to restrict processing, the right to data portability, the right to object and the right to withdraw consent. Because we do not keep your personal data on our own servers, the fastest options are:

You can always write to us at prvyucetjedna@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live.
